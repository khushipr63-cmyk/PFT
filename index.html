PURPOSE OF STUDY

The purpose of this study is to understand how gambling and substance use might affect how people learn and make decisions. You are being asked to take part in a research study. Before you decide to participate in this study, it is important that you understand why the research is being done and what it will involve. Please read the following information carefully. Please ask the researcher if there is anything that is not clear or if you need more information.

STUDY PROCEDURES

If you agree to participate in this study, you will complete two computer-based tasks remotely. The session will take approximately 20 minutes. During this session:

  • The researcher will explain the study again and answer any questions.
  • You will take part in a computer game where you choose between different options to try and earn points. Different choices may lead to better or worse outcomes. There are no right or wrong answers, and the goal is simply to make the best choices you can. Before the main task, you will complete practice rounds so you can understand how the task works.
  • At a later time, you will complete 2 very short computer-based tasks that measure attention, memory, and processing speed.
  • You may take breaks at any time during the session if needed.
  • After the session, you will be debriefed, and the study will be explained in more detail. You will also receive information about mental health and substance use support resources.

RISKS

There are no foreseeable risks associated with your participation in this study. If you anticipate any discomfort from the given information, please take a moment to reconsider your participation in this study.

BENEFITS

There are no direct benefits to participation, but you will help in increasing knowledge about how people who gamble or use multiple substances make decisions.

CONFIDENTIALITY

Your responses will be anonymous. Every effort will be made by the researcher to preserve your confidentiality, including the following:

  • Assigning code names/numbers for participants that will be used on all research notes and documents.
  • Keeping all data, contact information, and any other identifying participant information in a password-locked file with sole access to the researcher and supervisor.
  • In case of future publication, your individual data will not be shared with any third parties; only aggregated data results will be used with permission.

VOLUNTARY PARTICIPATION

Your participation in this study is voluntary. It is up to you to decide whether or not to take part in this study. If you decide to take part in this study and sign the consent form, you are still free to withdraw at any time and without giving a reason. Withdrawing from this study will not affect the relationship you have, if any, with the researcher. If you withdraw from the study before data collection is completed, your data will be returned to you or destroyed. To withdraw, please contact the principal investigator using the contact details provided above.
